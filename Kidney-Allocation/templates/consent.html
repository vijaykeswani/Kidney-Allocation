Research Participation Consent

Study

Kidney Organ Allocation Preference Study, conducted by Vijay Keswani (IIT Delhi).

What you will do

You will see pairs of patients who each need a kidney transplant and choose who you think should receive it. Afterwards, you will see a simple AI model — a computer program trained on your choices — that captures the pattern in them. You can rate it, test it, and edit it; then you lock it in and see how well it predicts a few more of your choices. The whole study takes about 20–25 minutes.

Timing

The study is untimed. Take as long as you need on each pair.

Risks and benefits

There are no anticipated risks beyond those of ordinary computer use. The study contributes to research on how people reason through organ allocation decisions when working alongside AI systems.
