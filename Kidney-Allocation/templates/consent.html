Key Information

Purpose

This research study is being conducted by {% if institute %}researchers at {{ institute }}{% else %}a team of researchers{% endif %}. We are interested in your opinions about, and experiences with, the allocation of medical resources. Your opinions and answers may inform the design of Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems that help humans make these types of decisions.

Procedures

We will ask you to complete some or all of the following activities:

  1. answer questions about yourself or other people
  2. answer questions about scenarios involving the use of AI
  3. share your opinions about how medical resources ought to be allocated.

We anticipate that the full experiment will take you approximately 20-30 minutes or less to complete.

Voluntariness

This experiment is completely voluntary. If at any time you desire to end a task or withdraw from this study, you may do so by closing your Internet browser window.

Confidentiality

We will not have access to any aspects of your personal information, such as your name or address, other than what you voluntarily contribute through your participation. De-identified information collected in this study may be used for internal research purposes.

Risks and benefits

It is possible that you might feel some discomfort upon reading or thinking about the stimuli in this study. There are no benefits to participating in this study.

To give your consent and participate in this research study, please press “Next” to continue.
