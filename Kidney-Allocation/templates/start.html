Organ allocation preference study

Kidneys for transplant are always scarcer than the patients who need them, so someone has to decide who receives one when it becomes available. This study looks at how people make that kind of call — not to find a "correct" answer, but to see whether we can build a simple model that captures the pattern in your choices.
